mathKAYA — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 September 2026
App: mathKAYA (package com.davincicodex.mathkaya)
Developer: DaVinci Codex
Contact: pelemervictor20@gmail.com

mathKAYA is a mathematics learning app for Grade 1–6 pupils and their teachers in the Philippines. This policy explains what information the app stores, why, and who can see it. We wrote it to be read by parents and teachers as well as reviewers.

1. What we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Account ID and passwordSign-upTo sign in. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider; we cannot read it.
First name, last name, nickname, grade level, chosen avatar, optional profile photoSign-up and profile editsTo greet the learner, show the correct grade-level content, and let teachers recognise their pupils.
Phone number and email addressSign-upAccount recovery and, for teachers, contact. For learner accounts we recommend a parent's or teacher's contact details, not the child's.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, device identifiers for advertising, or any usage analytics. The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs.

2. Optional AI features

Some features send short pieces of text to AI services through our own server so that the phone never holds any service keys:

  • Kuya Owl explanations and AI-made quizzes send the maths question, the chosen answer, the correct answer, the grade level and the language to a large language model (Anthropic Claude). No names, IDs or contact details are included.
  • Natural voices send the sentence to be spoken (for example a quiz question) to a text-to-speech service (ElevenLabs). The resulting audio is cached so the same sentence is generated only once.

If the device is offline, the app falls back to built-in explanations and the device's own voice.

3. Keeping accounts secure

To stop someone else signing in with a stolen password, the app stores a little extra information:

  • A random device code. When a second step is completed on a phone, that phone is remembered for 30 days so the step is not repeated every time. The code is generated at random on the device, is not derived from any hardware identifier, cannot be used to track anyone across apps or sites, and is never used for advertising. Only a one-way hash of it is stored. Clearing the app's data replaces it.
  • Sign-in records. The time and outcome of recent sign-in attempts, so the account owner can see them on the Security screen and so repeated wrong guesses can be slowed down. These are deleted automatically after 180 days.
  • A teacher's two-step sign-in. Teachers may turn on a 6-digit code from an authenticator app. The shared secret is held by our authentication provider (Supabase), not by us.
  • A learner's picture PIN. Children may choose a short sequence of pictures, asked for only on a phone they have not used before. Only a one-way hash is stored, never the pictures themselves, and a teacher can clear it if a child forgets. We do not store a phone number for this.

None of this is shared with anyone, and none of it is used for advertising, analytics or profiling.

4. Where data is stored

Account and classroom data are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. Access is restricted by row-level security: a learner can only read their own records and the classes they joined; a teacher can only read the classes they created and the pupils enrolled in them. All traffic uses HTTPS.

5. Children's privacy

mathKAYA is designed for use by children under 13 with the involvement of a teacher or parent. We collect only what is needed to run a classroom. We do not show ads, do not sell data, and do not use data for profiling. A parent or teacher may ask us at any time to review or delete a child's account by contacting the address above; we delete the account and all its records within 30 days.

6. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers named above (Supabase for hosting and authentication, the AI and voice providers for the optional features described in section 2), and only the minimum needed for each service to work. We never sell personal information.

7. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account exists. Deleting the app does not delete the account; ask us (or your teacher) to delete it — see how to delete your account. Cached voice audio contains no personal information and may be kept indefinitely.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed by the authentication provider. Service keys are held on our servers, not in the app. Data is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you we will notify affected users through the contact details on file.

9. Changes

We will post any changes to this page and update the effective date above.
