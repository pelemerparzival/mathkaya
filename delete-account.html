Delete your mathKAYA account

App: mathKAYA (package com.davincicodex.mathkaya)
Developer: DaVinci Codex

You can ask us to delete a mathKAYA account and all the data linked to it at any time. A parent, guardian or teacher may ask on behalf of a learner.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to pelemervictor20@gmail.com with the subject “mathKAYA account deletion”.
  2. Include the account's login ID (for example 2026-0001 or T-1234) and the full name on the account.
  3. If you are a parent, guardian or teacher asking for a learner, say so and give your relationship to them.
  4. We may reply to confirm the request before deleting, so that nobody can delete an account that isn't theirs.

Email a deletion request

We delete the account and its data within 30 days of confirming the request, and we email you when it is done. Uninstalling the app does not delete the account.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Login ID, password, name, nickname, grade, avatar and profile photo, phone number and emailDeleted
Practice progress (stars, coins, levels, activity log)Deleted
Class memberships, quiz answers, scores, solution-pad drawings and excuse notesDeleted
Two-step sign-in settings, picture PIN, remembered devices and sign-in recordsDeleted
Classes a teacher created, with their quizzes and lessonsDeleted with the teacher's account. Pupils enrolled in them lose access to that class.

What is kept

  • Cached voice audio of quiz questions and explanations. It contains no personal information and is shared by all users, so it is not linked to any account.
  • Server backups kept by our hosting provider may still hold deleted data for up to 30 days, until the backups roll over. They are not used for anything else.

Deleting some data without deleting the account

You can also ask us to delete only part of your data, such as a profile photo, phone number, email or a class's work, using the same email address. Say what you want deleted in your message.

See also the mathKAYA Privacy Policy.
